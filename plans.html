Custodial Support — FAQ

Custodial Support Plan — Frequently Asked Questions

Q: I already pay for domain, email, and hosting subscriptions. Why should I pay for custodial services too?

A: The Custodial Support Plan is not a replacement for those subscriptions — it’s an insurance-style service that protects access, continuity, and recovery. You keep paying subscription fees to registrars and hosting providers; this plan pays for secure storage, documented access procedures, and a verified handoff process so you won’t lose access or face long downtime if something goes wrong.

Q: What exactly does the plan cover?

A: We securely store your account access (where you authorize us), keep a documented record of credentials and recovery steps, perform periodic checks to verify access, and provide a controlled process to transfer access back to you or a designated party quickly and safely. It does not include active support, troubleshooting, or subscription fees.

Q: How does that help me in a real situation?

A: Common scenarios:

  • Someone leaves or forgets login details — we can get you back in without costly delays.
  • A registrar or host requires proof of ownership — our documented records speed verification.
  • Emergency handoff (illness, business sale, or transition) — we provide a secure, auditable transfer to the new administrator.
These reduce downtime, avoid lost revenue or broken email, and prevent expensive “account recovery” headaches.

Q: Isn’t this something I could do myself?

A: You can maintain credentials yourself, but many businesses struggle with secure storage, up-to-date documentation, and reliable off-site recovery. We provide a consistent, secure process and an auditable agreement that third parties (hosts, registrars, buyers) respect. That consistency is the main value.

Q: How is access stored and protected?

A: Access is stored per a documented agreement with you; credentials are encrypted, access is limited to authorized personnel, and we maintain a clear change log. We recommend adding 2FA/secondary protections where supported — in many cases we store recovery steps or emergency codes instead of 2FA tokens.

Q: Will you change my subscriptions or billing?

A: No. You remain the account owner and continue to pay any subscription invoices. Our role is custodial: we hold and safeguard the access information and manage handoffs per the agreement. Any changes to accounts or billing require your prior written approval.

Q: What about security and liability?

A: Security is handled according to the agreement: encrypted storage, least-privilege access, and documented handoff procedures so expectations are clear. The agreement spells out responsibilities and limits of liability. If you’d like, we can include required notification timelines or an escrow-style release process in the contract.

Q: How do I stop the service or get my credentials back?

A: You can terminate the custodial agreement at any time (see contract for notice period). At termination we’ll securely return credentials, revoke any delegated access we hold, and provide a final transfer report.

Q: Who should consider this plan?

A: Small businesses, busy owners, and any organization that values continuity — especially those with single-person admins, infrequent logins, or third-party dependencies (e.g., website builders, email providers). It’s also useful when preparing for a sale or handing the business to a new manager.

Short summary: Custodial Support: $30.00/month — Securely store and document your domain, email, and site access so you can recover or transfer control quickly and cleanly; subscription fees remain your responsibility.

Plans — FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions — Service Plans

Q: How do I choose between Pay-As-You-Go and a subscription plan?

A: Choose Pay-As-You-Go if you only need occasional help — it’s best for one-off diagnostics and repairs. Choose a subscription (Priority Care or Priority Support) if you want faster scheduling, reduced diagnostic costs, and predictable monthly budgeting. If you need hardware procurement and installation as a focus, pick the Hardware-First option.

Q: What does the Initial Diagnostic Assessment include and why is it charged?

A: The $75 Initial Diagnostic Assessment covers structured AREAR troubleshooting: collecting symptoms, testing likely causes, and producing a clear report with recommended next steps and pricing. Charging for diagnosis prevents unnecessary work and makes sure any major repairs are approved before they begin.

Q: What are the main differences between Priority Care ($20–$40/mo) and Priority Support ($60–$120+/mo)?

A: Priority Care is geared toward home users — lower monthly cost, reduced diagnostic fees, and faster scheduling. Priority Support is designed for small businesses — higher priority response, more included triage time, faster escalation paths, and potentially waived diagnostic fees. Exact pricing and included features are scoped at enrollment.

Q: If I have a subscription, do I still pay for parts or major repairs?

A: Yes. Subscriptions cover diagnostic and scheduling priority, not the cost of replacement parts or major labor unless specifically included in a custom plan. Hardware and major repair costs are quoted separately and require your approval before work proceeds.

Q: What is the Hardware-First Support Option and who should use it?

A: The Hardware-First option is for clients prioritizing upgrades, replacements, or new hardware installs. We can source hardware at cost + handling or work with customer-provided parts, then install and configure. Use this if most of your needs are equipment-focused rather than troubleshooting software or networking issues.

Q: How does scheduling and response time work for subscription plans?

A: Subscriptions give you priority scheduling over non-members. Exact response windows depend on plan level and current workload; Priority Support receives the fastest response, followed by Priority Care. We’ll confirm typical response expectations during enrollment.

Q: Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan?

A: Yes — plans can be changed. Upgrades take effect immediately (billing prorated as applicable). Downgrades are scheduled per the contract terms. We’ll walk through timing and billing at the time of the change.

Q: Are there any long-term contracts or commitments?

A: Standard subscriptions are month-to-month unless we agree on a longer-term contract. Any contract terms (discounts for annual payment, minimum terms, etc.) will be made explicit during enrollment.

Q: What’s not included in subscriptions?

A: Subscriptions do not automatically include the cost of hardware, third-party subscription fees (domains, hosting, SaaS), or extended project work beyond incident triage unless explicitly specified. They also do not cover on-site parts shipped at cost unless included in a custom plan. All major work requires approval and will be quoted separately.

Q: How do I enroll or ask questions before subscribing?

A: Use the Subscribe page to request enrollment, or call 512-540-6522 to discuss needs and find the right plan. We’ll confirm scope, pricing, and any onboarding steps before activation.

Short summary: Pay-As-You-Go covers one-off diagnostics and repairs. Priority Care ($20–$40/mo) is for home users who want lower fees and faster scheduling. Priority Support ($60–$120+/mo) is for business-critical systems needing faster response and more triage time. Hardware-First handles procurement and installs. Subscriptions do not include third-party fees or hardware unless specified.
