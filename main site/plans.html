Priority Care Plan

Monthly Plan: $20-$40

Includes:

Priority Support Plan

Best for small businesses or clients who rely heavily on their systems.

Monthly Plan: $60-$120+ (based on needs)

Includes:

  • Reduced or waived diagnostic fee
  • Priority response and scheduling
  • Discounted labor rates
  • Structured AREAR troubleshooting with faster escalation
  • Limited triage time per incident included

All major work requires approval before execution.

Hardware-First Support Option

For clients focused on upgrades or replacements.

Includes:

  • Hardware supplied at cost + handling margin, or customer-provided parts
  • Installation and configuration support
  • Compatibility guidance before purchase
  • Discounted or standard labor depending on plan

Labor is always billed separately from hardware unless otherwise specified in a service plan.

Service Standard

Applies to all plans.

All work follows the AREAR troubleshooting workflow:

Assess -> Research -> Explore -> Apply -> Resolve

  • No guessing or blind repair work
  • All major work requires approval after diagnosis
  • Clear communication of findings before escalation
